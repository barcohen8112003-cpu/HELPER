About

Helper is a private, non-commercial assistant built and used by a single individual for his own accounts. It helps him keep track of his own school assignments, calendar, tasks and email, and sends him reminders through his own WhatsApp chat.

Helper is not offered to the public, has no other users, and no one can sign up for it.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

In short: Helper only accesses the Google data of its owner, only to help the owner, keeps it on the owner's own computer, and never sells, shares or transfers it to anyone.

Who uses this app

The only user of Helper is its developer, who connects his own Google accounts. No data of any other person is collected.

Google data the app accesses

  • Gmail: reads messages to notify the owner of important mail, and sends email only after the owner explicitly approves each message.
  • Google Calendar: reads events and adds events the owner asks for.
  • Google Tasks: reads and adds the owner's tasks.
  • Google Drive: read-only access to files the owner asks about.
  • Google Classroom: read-only access to the owner's courses, assignments and submission status.

How the data is used

The data is used only to provide features to the owner: reminders, summaries, answers to his questions and notifications about his own assignments, events and messages. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and the assistant's working data are stored locally on the owner's own computer. To answer a request, relevant text may be sent to the AI model provider the owner configured, solely to produce that answer.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, rented or shared with third parties, and never transferred except as needed to provide the features above to the owner himself.

Limited Use

Helper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data and revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing an account from Helper deletes its stored token from the owner's computer.
